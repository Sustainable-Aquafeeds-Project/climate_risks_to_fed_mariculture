# Production troubleshooting notes

- Farm 95751 is japanese_amberjack in the United Arab Emirates (ARE). Farm is way too hot - fish could not feed for ~1/3 of growing time. It's the only farm growing that species in UAE and their annual reported output was ~100t, so let's just exclude that farm.

- Farms 9221, 9432, 9279, 9528, 9644, 9694, 9775, 9861, 9901, 9983, and 10338 are rainbow_trout in Japan (JPN). 
		- Farms 9221, 9279, 9432, 9775, 9861, 9901, and 9983 could not grow more than 100g in 2025. These should be excluded.
		- Farms 9528, 9644, 9694, and 10338 were able to reach a harvest weight os > 200g for harvest, which I'm going to consider "acceptable". These can stay.
	As it stands, the production per farm is Japan is on the lower side (950 t, while some other places are > 2000). Excluding the 6 farms above will put the production per farm at 1388 t, so that is fine.

- Farms 8834 and 8843 are seabream in Iran. They are the only two farms for seabream in Iran and both failed to grow to >100g in 2025 as the farms were too hot. They will be excluded - while this will exclude all of Iran's seabream, it's 2.8% of Iran's total aquaculture. Iran's "seabream" aquaculture is made up of a tropical species (_Acanthopagrus bifasciatus_) so the temperature limits of _Sparus aurata_ may not fit them so well anyway.
